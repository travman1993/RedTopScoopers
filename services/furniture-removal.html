The Heaviest, Most Awkward Stuff in the House

Furniture is the number-one reason people call a junk removal company. It makes sense. A sleeper sofa can weigh 200 pounds or more. Sectionals come apart into pieces that still don't fit in an SUV. Solid-wood dressers and armoires are built to outlast everyone in the house. And getting any of it out means pivoting through door frames, down stairwells, and around corners without gouging the walls.

Red Top Scoopers handles furniture removal across Cartersville, Bartow County, Northwest Georgia, and Metro Atlanta. We send a crew that does this every day. They know how to tip, angle, and pivot big pieces out of tight spaces, and sometimes how to break them down safely when they won't come out whole.

Donate It, Sell It, or Have It Hauled?

Good furniture deserves a second life, and we'll donate usable pieces whenever we can. But it's worth being honest about how the "just sell it" plan usually plays out. You photograph the couch and list it online. You field a dozen "is this still available?" messages. Then you wait for someone who no-shows twice and finally offers a third of your asking price. And most donation centers won't take upholstered furniture with stains, tears, or pet damage. Many won't take mattresses at all.

We wrote a full guide on this: sell it, donate it, or haul it away? The short version is that your time has value. Sometimes paying to make it disappear today beats spending three weekends trying to get $40 for it.

Mattress Removal

Mattresses are bulky and awkward to carry. Most charities won't accept used ones, and they're a pain to fit in a personal vehicle. If you just got a new mattress delivered and the store won't take the old one, or the old set has been leaning against the garage wall for a month, we'll take it. Mattress pickups are often combined with other furniture to make the most of a single trip.

Protecting Your Home on the Way Out

Big furniture plus narrow hallways is how walls get scuffed. Our crew takes the time to plan the path out, remove legs or cushions when it helps, and carry rather than drag. It's one of the core values we hold ourselves to. We'd rather take an extra five minutes than leave a mark.

Furniture Removal Pricing

Furniture is priced by how much space it takes in the truck and how hard it is to get out. A loveseat on a ground-floor porch costs less than a sleeper sofa from a third-floor apartment. Send photos by text to 404-649-4654 for a fast, upfront quote. Read more about how junk removal pricing works.

Common Questions

Do you take couches with stains, tears, or pet damage?

Yes. Furniture doesn't have to be in good shape for us to haul it. Pieces in good condition get donated when possible; the rest is disposed of responsibly.

Can you take a sectional or sleeper sofa from upstairs?

Yes. Our crew regularly removes heavy furniture from upper floors and basements. If a piece won't fit through the doorway whole, we can often disassemble it.

Do you take mattresses?

Yes, all sizes, along with box springs, bed frames, and adjustable bases.
