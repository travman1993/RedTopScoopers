Junk Removal That Starts and Ends at Your Front Door

Most households don't have a "junk problem" so much as a junk backlog. It's the treadmill nobody's touched since 2019, the box spring that didn't fit up the new stairway, the bags from a closet purge that never made it to the donation center, and the broken patio set that's been waiting for "a free weekend." None of it is an emergency on its own. Together, it takes over a room, a garage bay, or the back corner of the yard.

Red Top Scoopers LLC is a residential junk removal company based in Cartersville, GA. We serve homeowners, renters, landlords, and families across Bartow County, Northwest Georgia, and Metro Atlanta. You point; we lift, carry, load, haul, and clean up. You don't need to drag anything to the curb, rent a trailer, or borrow a friend's truck. We bring the truck, the crew, and the muscle.

What Residential Junk Removal Includes

Our residential service is full-service. That means:

  • Removal from anywhere on the property — upstairs bedrooms, basements, crawl-space entries, attics, back decks, sheds, and side yards.
  • All loading by our crew. You don't need to stage or bag anything (though if you've already bagged it, great).
  • Careful carrying. We protect door frames, banisters, and floors on the way out. Read more about how we respect your property.
  • Sweep-up when we're done. The spot where the junk was gets left broom-clean.
  • Responsible disposal. Usable items get set aside for donation and recycling whenever possible. Only what truly can't be reused goes to the landfill.

Single-Item Pickup vs. Whole-Home Cleanout

There's no job too small. A lot of our residential calls are for one item: a sleeper sofa, an old chest freezer, a piano-weight entertainment center. These are things that are simply too big or too heavy to handle yourself. Single-item pickups are often the fastest to schedule because they fit around larger jobs.

On the other end are whole-home cleanouts. These come up when someone is moving, downsizing, getting a rental ready for new tenants, or settling a loved one's estate. They can take several truckloads. For those, we'll talk through what's staying, what's being donated, and what's going, so nothing important leaves by mistake. If you're handling a family member's home, our estate cleanout service is built for that situation.

How Residential Pricing Works

We don't charge by the hour and we don't post a one-size-fits-all price list. Junk removal cost comes down to how much space your items take up in the truck, how heavy they are, and how hard they are to get to. A single recliner by the front door is priced very differently from a basement full of furniture down a narrow stairwell.

Text us a few photos at 404-649-4654 and we'll give you an upfront quote. The price we agree on is the price you pay. For the full breakdown, read how much junk removal costs and our upfront pricing promise.

Why Homeowners Hire Instead of DIY

The do-it-yourself route sounds cheaper until you add it up. There's the truck or trailer rental, the gas, the landfill tipping fee, a few hours of your Saturday, a sore back, and a real chance of dinging a wall or a door frame. Then there's the wait in line at the transfer station. We wrote a whole guide on skipping the landfill trip. Another one covers when it's smarter to pay for removal than to try selling it.

There's also a reason that's harder to put a price on. A cleared-out room just feels different. If you've been living around a pile of stuff you've been meaning to deal with, you know the low-level stress it creates. Our guide on decluttering and mental health gets into why.

Common Questions

Do I have to carry anything outside before you arrive?

No. Residential junk removal is full-service — our crew removes items from wherever they are in your home, garage, attic, or yard.

Can you pick up just one item?

Yes. Single-item pickups like a couch, mattress, or appliance are some of our most common jobs, and they're often easy to fit into the schedule quickly.

Will you take items from a second floor or basement?

Yes. Stairs, basements, and tight hallways are part of the job. Access does affect the quote, so mention it (or send photos) when you call or text.
