FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking junk removal with Red Top Scoopers LLC. Still have a question? Call or text us.

What kind of junk do you take?

Furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, garage and attic clutter, construction and renovation debris, yard waste, storm debris, and full property cleanouts. If it's junk, we probably haul it — call or text and ask.

What items can't you take?

We can't take hazardous materials such as chemicals, paint, asbestos, medical waste, or similar items. If you're not sure whether something qualifies, call or text us a description or photo and we'll let you know.

How much will it cost?

Junk removal pricing depends on volume, weight, and how accessible the items are, so we don't post fixed prices. Call or text us a description or a few photos and we'll give you a straight, upfront quote before we start — no hidden fees.

Do I need to be home during the pickup?

Not always. As long as the items are accessible and we've agreed on the scope and price ahead of time, we can often complete a job without you being on-site. We'll work out the details when you call or text.

How fast can you come out?

We offer same-day and next-day junk removal in most of our service area, depending on our schedule. Call or text us and we'll let you know the soonest available slot.

What areas do you service?

We're based in Cartersville, GA and serve Rome, Acworth, Kennesaw, Woodstock, Marietta, Dallas, Rockmart, and the surrounding Northwest Georgia and Metro Atlanta area. Not sure if you're in range? Call or text us your city or zip code.

Do I need to move items to the curb myself?

No. Full-service loading is included — our crew comes inside, loads everything, and sweeps up afterward. You just point to what needs to go.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Red Top Scoopers LLC is fully licensed and insured for every job we take on.

How do I book a pickup?

Just call or text 404-649-4654. Tell us what you need hauled and your location, and we'll get you a quote and a time on the schedule.
